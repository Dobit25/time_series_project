\abstract{
This research paper examines the price elasticity and social contagion effects of two archetypal games: a single-player game (Elden Ring) and a multiplayer game (Ready or Not). Using a Dynamic OLS approach with HC3 robust standard errors, we investigate the validity of the "Instant Play" hypothesis for multiplayer games and the "Stockpiling" hypothesis for single-player games.
}
